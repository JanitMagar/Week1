Understanding HTML Links

Hyperlinks are the backbone of the web. They allow users to navigate between pages seamlessly. Below, we explore the two main types of links: Absolute and Relative.

1. Absolute Linking

Absolute links point to a specific, full URL on the internet. They must include the protocol (like https://) and the domain name. These are typically used to link to external websites.

Example: Learn more about web development by visiting the Wikipedia page for HTTP.

2. Relative Linking

Relative links point to a file relative to the current file's location on the same server. They do not include the protocol or domain name. These are ideal for linking pages within your own website
because if you change your domain name, the links won't break.

Examples:
  • Read our About Us page to meet the team.
  • Check out our Privacy Policy to see how we handle data.
